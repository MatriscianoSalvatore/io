Side projects, over the years

In July 2016 I learned Android development and made my very first Android app: a simple compass app pointing north with a nice user interface.
From July to September 2017 I developed an Android app for a virtual tour guide of Bologna called Visitup Bologna — and in 2025 I took over the entire project from the previous owner and created a new version of the app using Flutter (iOS, Android, Web).
In July 2018 I made a remote control Android app for an Arduino and Raspberry Pi car using TCP Socket, and from February to October 2019 I made Keiken: an Android app that let users book "experiences" created by other users.

From July to September 2026 I made AI Pantry: an Android app with a fully on-device AI pipeline — you photograph your fridge, Gemini Nano or MobileCLIP-S2 recognise the ingredients and Gemma 4 E2B generates the recipes, with no internet permission at all.

AI Pantry — Android app

Milano, Italy
  • Kotlin
  • Jetpack Compose
  • Gemini Nano
  • LiteRT
  • Gemma
  • CameraX
  • Room
  • Koin
  • Project, design and development of the AI Pantry Android app, a Droidcon demo of a fully on-device AI pipeline: you photograph your fridge and pantry, the app recognises the ingredients and generates recipes that use only what you have. Gemini Nano (AICore, via the ML Kit GenAI Prompt API) and MobileCLIP-S2 zero-shot on LiteRT detect the ingredients, Gemma 4 E2B on LiteRT-LM generates the recipes. Inference is 100% on-device and the app does not even hold the INTERNET permission: the ~2.5 GB model is delivered through Play for On-device AI for the Play Store release, or embedded in the APK for testing. Clean Architecture and MVI with Koin, CameraX and Room, with a persistent GPU/CPU fallback policy for the LLM runtime.
